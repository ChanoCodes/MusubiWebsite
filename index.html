Our Story

Born in the heart of Libon, Albay, our Musubi brings together traditional Japanese flavors with Filipino warmth. Every bite tells a story of passion, quality, and community.

From Libon, Albay

Musubi started as a small idea by a cousin here in Libon, Albay.

After a few months, it grew into something we both believed in.

What began as a simple food experiment became a shared passion — combining Japanese-inspired comfort food with our own local touch.
